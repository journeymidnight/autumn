  per burst into one tcp_sendmsg.

  RpcConn writer_task (root cause unclear — mpsc reservation pool
  exhaustion or compio SQ overflow). Cap=4 keeps 256×4=1024 concurrent
  futures in bound with WRITE_CHANNEL_CAP=1024.
